# Morning run

Moderate aerobic run before breakfast with residual insulin sensitization through the morning.

## Scenario inputs

- meals (h): [8, 12.5, 18.5]
- meal widths taug (h): [0.9, 1, 1.1]
- activity pulses (center, width, amplitude): [(6.75, 0.55, 0.55)]
- activity enabled: yes
- A_raw max: 0.550
- normalized activity proxy max: 0.550
- activity params: k_A=5, A_thresh=0.3, gamma_A=0.18, kappa_A=0.5, beta_A=0.15, eta_A=0.55, tau_A=3

## Physiological rationale

1. **Run intensity and direct uptake.** The activity pulse peaks at a pre-normalized intensity of 0.55 to represent a moderate run, which is strong enough to trigger contraction-mediated GLUT4 translocation without treating the session as maximal exercise. gamma_A is increased to 0.18 to make the acute glucose sink slightly stronger than the default sedentary configuration.
   - Richter EA, Hargreaves M. Exercise, GLUT4, and skeletal muscle glucose uptake. Physiol Rev. 2013;93(3):993-1017. PMID: 23899560.
   - Lund S, Holman GD, Schmitz O, Pedersen O. Contraction stimulates translocation of glucose transporter GLUT4 in skeletal muscle through a mechanism distinct from that of insulin. Proc Natl Acad Sci U S A. 1995;92(13):5817-5821. PMID: 7597034.
2. **Post-exercise insulin sensitivity.** eta_A is raised to 0.55 and tau_A to 3.0 h so the model keeps a noticeable post-run insulin-sensitizing tail through breakfast and into lunch, matching the well-described several-hour acute improvement after one aerobic bout.
   - Cartee GD. Mechanisms for greater insulin-stimulated glucose uptake in normal and insulin-resistant skeletal muscle after acute exercise. Am J Physiol Endocrinol Metab. 2015;309(12):E949-E959. PMID: 26487009.
   - Bird SR, Hawley JA. Update on the effects of physical activity on insulin sensitivity in humans. BMJ Open Sport Exerc Med. 2017;2(1):e000143. PMID: 28879003.
   - Short KR, Pratt LV, Teague AM, et al. A single exercise session increases insulin sensitivity in normal weight and overweight/obese adolescents. Pediatr Diabetes. 2019;20(8):1073-1082. PMID: 30378106.
3. **Counter-regulatory cortisol kept modest.** beta_A is held to 0.15 so the run produces a visible but not dominant counter-regulatory cortisol bump; moderate aerobic exercise tends to raise cortisol once intensity passes a threshold, but the glucose-lowering effect of muscle uptake still predominates in short runs.
   - Hill EE, Zack E, Battaglini C, Viru M, Viru A, Hackney AC. Exercise and circulating cortisol levels: the intensity threshold effect. J Endocrinol Invest. 2008;31(7):587-591. PMID: 18787373.

## Scope note

This demo models the run as a glucose-disposal and short-lived sensitization event. The engine still has no explicit hepatic glucose production term, so prolonged fasting runs would overstate glucose lowering.
